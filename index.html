ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Turning Food Surplus into Social Impact

Food waste and food scarcity can exist side by side. Often, surplus food is discarded simply because there is no efficient way to connect it with those who need it.

The Problem

Restaurants, hotels, and canteens may have surplus edible food, while vulnerable communities face food insecurity.

Our Solution

A digital platform that connects food providers with verified NGOs and charitable organizations.

Our Mission

Reduce avoidable food waste and make safe surplus food easier to redistribute responsibly.

A SIMPLE, CONNECTED PROCESS

How It Works

01

Donors List Food

Verified food providers publish surplus food details, quantities, safety information, and pickup deadlines.

02

NGOs Request Food

Verified recipient organizations find suitable donations and submit pickup requests.

03

Coordinate Collection

Donors confirm requests, recipients arrange collection, and the platform tracks donation progress.

04

Admins Oversee

Administrators review organizations, monitor activity, and handle reported issues.

BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE REDISTRIBUTION

Features That Make a Difference

📍 Location-Based Matching

Help recipients discover donations within a suitable collection area.

🤝 Donation Requests

Enable NGOs to request food and donors to respond to requests.

🚚 Pickup Tracking

Track donations from availability through successful completion.

🛡️ Organization Verification

Review donor and recipient documents before enabling core features.

🥗 Food Safety Information

Record preparation times, storage conditions, allergens, and consumption deadlines.

📊 Impact Monitoring

Measure completed donations, food redistributed, and estimated meals.
